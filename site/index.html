Fifty portals is not a buying process.

Every manufacturer gives your branch its own price, in its own portal, behind its own login. So buyers click through tabs, call reps and quote from last quarter's price book.

Today

  • Log into each brand's portal, one at a time.
  • Copy prices into a spreadsheet by hand.
  • Call the rep to ask what's in stock.
  • Quote the customer and hope it holds.

With Onecounter

  • Type the part once.
  • See every brand's version side by side, at your price.
  • See stock and the arrival date on the same line.
  • Copy the quote sheet and send it.

How it works

Setup takes one visit per brand. After that, nobody on your team opens a manufacturer portal to check a price again.

Step 1

Connect each brand once

Sign in to each manufacturer account you already have. Your price tier comes with it.

Step 2

Search one box

Part number, name or keyword. Onecounter asks every connected brand at the same time.

Step 3

Quote and order

Build the quote sheet, send it to the customer, then order the usual way.
